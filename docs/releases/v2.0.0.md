# Hydronicus 2.0.0

Room influence becomes a slow, capped, self-correcting room trim, so a heating curve that is set a little too low or too high is corrected over days without slow floors and ceilings swinging.
It changes what an existing `room_influence` without the new keys does, so this is a major release; the last section says how to keep the old behaviour.

## What changed

- The lead zone's error passes through a first-order filter, `smoothing`, 3600 seconds by default, and counts as none within a `deadband`, 0.2 K by default; only its part beyond the deadband trims.
- A learned part integrates that error at `learn_rate`, 0.3 K per hour per kelvin by default, up to `learn_limit`, 2 K by default, and is kept across restarts; `learn_rate: 0` turns it off.
- The learned part rises only once the lead zone has called without a pause for `learn_after`, 28800 seconds by default, falls while no lead zone calls as if the room were the deadband above its target, and learns nothing more than 1 K from the target.
- It holds its value during a warm-up, hot water boosts and disinfection, a held off heating request, Dry run, and whenever the Plant does not heat on Hydronicus's curve, and it never winds up beyond the trim's limit or the supply's bounds.
- `limit` now caps both parts together, and its default drops from 3 K to 2 K.
- A heating supply target with a `step` moves only once it has left the last proposal by 0.6 of a step, so it no longer flips between two neighbouring degrees.
- The new **Room trim** sensor shows the trim in K, with its proportional and learned parts, the lead zone, and why learning is held; the **Reset room trim** button forgets the learned part.
- The supply reason now reads the sum, such as `supply target 37.0 °C: curve 35.0 °C, offset +0.0 K, room trim +1.7 K (learned +1.4 K)`.
- The Status sensor's `heating_curve` attribute renames `room_shift` to `room_trim` and adds `curve` and `learned`.

## Upgrading

A plant file with `room_influence` still imports, and its room trim now responds over hours instead of at once, within 2 K instead of 3 K, and learns.
To keep the old instant, proportional shift, add `limit: 3`, `smoothing: 0`, `deadband: 0`, and `learn_rate: 0` to `room_influence`.
Automations and dashboards that read `heating_curve.room_shift` from the Status sensor read `heating_curve.room_trim` instead.

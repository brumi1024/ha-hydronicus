# Hydronicus 1.2.0

A warm-up holds a fixed supply temperature, settable from the dashboard.
By the versioning rule in CONTRIBUTING.md, removing the warm-up's `shift` and `curve_steps` and the curve number's `hotter` would make this a major release; it ships as 1.2.0 because those keys came out in 1.1.0 the same day.

## What changed

- A warm-up holds the heating supply at least at a warm-up temperature for its time, under every heating supply strategy: a fixed temperature or Hydronicus's curve is raised to it, and the heat pump's own curve is turned off for the warm-up while the supply setpoint holds it, then turned on again.
- The new **Warm-up temperature** number sets that temperature; it starts from `source.warm_up.temperature` (default 40 °C), keeps its value across restarts, and a change applies to a running warm-up at once.
- The `hydronicus.warm_up` action takes an optional `temperature` for that warm-up only, in place of `shift`; the **Warm-up** switch shows `until`, `temperature`, and `zones`.
- A warm-up needs the source's supply setpoint and is refused without one; it no longer touches the curve number, and it ends when the heat pump's curve is turned on elsewhere.

## What to change in a plant file

Before importing a 1.1.0 plant file again:

- Remove `hotter` from `source.weather_curve.curve_number`.
- Replace `source.warm_up.shift` and `source.warm_up.curve_steps` with `source.warm_up.temperature`, the supply temperature in °C a warm-up holds at least, within the supply's `minimum` and `maximum`.

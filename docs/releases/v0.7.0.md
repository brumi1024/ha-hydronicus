# Hydronicus 0.7.0

This release teaches Hydronicus to run a heat pump whose every stop restarts its own pumps, through a bridge that limits and sometimes refuses its writes, and to own the heat pump's hot water.
Every new setting is optional; a Plant that sets none of them behaves as before.

## Fewer stops for a heat pump

- `hold_after_demand` keeps the source on into its `heat_sink_loops` after the last zone is satisfied, so a new call within the hold needs no new start.
- `heat_sink_overshoot` lets a heat sink loop take heat beyond the minimum run only while its zone is less than that many kelvin over its target; the source is released once none has room.
- `max_starts_per_hour`, `start_threshold`, and loop `weight` limit how often the source starts.

## Health, handover, and other sources

- `health_sensor` releases the source at once while it or its link is not healthy, with the source's **Fault** binary sensor and the `source_unhealthy` Repair.
- `handover_sensor` hands the source to its own controller: Hydronicus sends it nothing and keeps a heat sink open while it may heat.
- `inhibit_sensor`, a binary sensor or an input boolean, keeps the source off while another source, such as a fireplace's buffer, supplies the zones; the zones go on circulating.
- The `no_load` guard releases and holds off a heat pump whose compressor runs while its water barely changes temperature, and raises the `source_no_load` Repair if it happens twice in a day.
- A source request found on with no open loop is now reported even behind a separator.

## Writing through a bridge

- `write_interval` keeps writes to the source's outputs apart, for a bridge that refuses faster ones; only the request or the DHW switch turning off is never held.
- Three writes that are not taken raise the output's Repair and then retry hourly instead of in a loop.

## Cooling and supply control

- `supply.modes` limits the supply setpoint to the modes Hydronicus owns it in, such as cooling only, and `supply.step` rounds it to the setpoint's resolution, upwards while cooling.
- The setpoint waits until the mode select shows the mode.
- `mode_switches` sets switches that follow the mode, such as a weather curve that heating uses and cooling does not.

## Hot water

- The source's `dhw` binds its DHW switch and setpoint, which Hydronicus owns and shows as the **DHW** switch and the **DHW target** number, kept within hard limits.
- A change made on the source's own controller is adopted, never written back.
- The `hydronicus.boost_dhw` action raises the target for a bounded time and restores it, also after a restart or a lost link; `hydronicus.end_dhw_boost` ends it early.

## Diagnostics

- With a `compressor_sensor`, the source gets **Compressor starts** within the last hour and the mean **Compressor cycle**.

The cycling protection form now also offers **Hold after demand**, **Heat sink overshoot**, and **Source handover sensor**; the other new settings are plant file keys.
See [the source](../plant-file.md#source) for every key.

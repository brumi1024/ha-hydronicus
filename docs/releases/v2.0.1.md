# Hydronicus 2.0.1

The room trim's learned part no longer learns while the Plant is away, so the house no longer comes back from a holiday on a heating curve taught to be too cold.

## What changed

- While the Plant is away, until its return time or until it is ended, the learned part keeps its value, and the **Room trim** sensor's **Learning held** says `the Plant is away`.
  Before, rooms settled at their away level taught the curve's line, which is set for comfort, to drop by up to `learn_limit`, and that took days to unlearn.
- The same hold applies while the lead zone holds its own away level, picked as its preset or by its schedule, without the Plant being away; Learning held then says `the lead zone is away`.
- An early return from away is part of the away, and the recovery after it is a change of the lead zone's target, which already teaches the learned part nothing, so it continues from where it was before the away.
- The proportional part still follows the lead zone's error against its away target, as it does for a setback.

A reset of the room trim at the end of an away is no longer needed and discards what was learned before it.
Every 2.0.0 plant file imports unchanged.

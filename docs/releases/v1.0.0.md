# Hydronicus 1.0.0

The first public release.
Hydronicus runs a water-based heating and cooling plant from Home Assistant: it opens valves, runs pumps, and asks the heat pump or boiler for heat or cooling in a safe order, and checks that every step really happened.

## What it does

- One Plant per system: an optional source, its pumps, zones over Home Assistant areas, and plant loops, set up with guided setup or a plant file.
- A safe start and stop sequence with minimum flow protection, minimum on and off times, overrun, and post-run.
- Heating and cooling on the same loops, a mode dwell between them, and an optional automatic changeover.
- A condensation guard on every cooling loop from the worst-case dew point, with optional condensation switches, surface minimums, and humidity limits.
- Digital thermostats with presets, comfort schedules, bounded early start, optional learned recovery and weather-aware recovery, windows, and away.
- Supply control with fixed targets or an outdoor heating curve, and ownership of the source's hot water with a schedule and a disinfection cycle.
- Frost protection, weekly exercise of idle pumps and valves, a zone response check, and a sensor fallback.
- Confirmed commands with retries and Repairs, arming per output, and Dry run until you turn on **Control equipment**.
- A first heat pump adapter: a Shelly Modbus bridge for Midea heat pumps.

## Requirements

Hydronicus requires Home Assistant 2026.9.0 or newer, installed through HACS as a custom repository.

## Upgrading from a pre-release

Pre-release Plants are not migrated.
Export each Plant with the `hydronicus.export_plant` action, remove it, update, and import the file again; check it against [the full example](../examples/full-plant.yaml).

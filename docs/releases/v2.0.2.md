# Hydronicus 2.0.2

The room trim's learned part now climbs back by itself when the weather turns cold, so a curve it lowered on mild days no longer needs **Reset room trim**.

## What changed

- The wait before the learned part rises, `learn_after`, now counts how long the lead zones together have called without a moment when none of them called.
  Before, it counted only the current lead zone's own call, so every change of lead zone and every pause of one zone while the other kept calling started the wait again.
  With two slow zones taking turns to lead, the learned part could almost never rise, while it kept falling in every hour that no zone called.
- A change of lead zone no longer counts as a change of target when the two zones keep different targets, such as 22 °C and 21.5 °C.
  Each lead zone's target is remembered from when it last led, and only a zone that leads with another target than then settles.
  Before, every turn between two such zones started a settling, which held the learned part while the room's error moved and kept it from falling for `learn_after` after each turn.
- Whether the room still warms up, which holds the learned part, is now judged on the lead zone's own error, which each lead zone filters for itself while another leads.
  Before, it was judged on the trim's filter, which blends the two zones' errors for an hour after every change of lead, so each turn between zones whose errors differ looked like a warm-up and held the learned part for two hours.
- The **Room trim** sensor's **Learning held** now says `the lead zones have not called long enough` for that wait.
- The learned part still falls only while no lead zone calls, and a zone that just resumed and still warms up still teaches it nothing.
- A valve, a switched pump, or the source's request switch that goes unavailable for less than a minute now keeps its last known state, so one whose connection drops for a second no longer releases the source.
  Before, a valve that was unavailable for half a second took its loop's path away, which released a heat pump that was heating every zone and then held it off for its minimum off time, a compressor stop and a pump restart for nothing.
  One unavailable for a minute or longer is still taken as unusable, and its loop or the source stops as before.

Every 2.0.1 plant file imports unchanged.

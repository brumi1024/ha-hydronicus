# Hydronicus 0.6.0

This release lets a comfort schedule hold more than one level a day, and gives dashboards everything they need to show and edit it.

## Schedule levels

- A block of the comfort schedule helper may name its level in its data: a `preset` (`comfort`, `eco`, or `away`), or a `heat_target` and `cool_target`.
  A block without these keys still holds the manual comfort target.
- Early start now recovers towards the next period's level, including from a lower block to a higher one, not only from a setback to comfort.
- Hydronicus reads the helper's week with the `schedule.get_schedule` action whenever the helper changes, so an edit to a later block takes effect at once.
- A block with unusable data holds the manual comfort target, sets `schedule_status` to `invalid_block`, and raises the new `invalid_schedule_block` Repair, which lists each such block.

See [block levels](../how-it-works.md#block-levels).

## Setbacks on the dashboard

- Each zone with a comfort schedule has **Heating setback** and **Cooling setback** numbers, which start from the configured `heat_setback` and `cool_setback` and keep their value across restarts.
- Setbacks are now limited to 10 K, in the plant file, the forms, and the numbers.

## For dashboards

- The thermostat adds `schedule_entity`, `schedule_level`, and `schedule_next_change`, so a card needs only the thermostat to find the helper and say what applies now and when that changes.

See [editing a schedule from a dashboard](../how-it-works.md#editing-a-schedule-from-a-dashboard).

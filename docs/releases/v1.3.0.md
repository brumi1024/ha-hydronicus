# Hydronicus 1.3.0

A supply can bound heating more narrowly than cooling, so one supply covers a heat pump whose heating and cooling setpoints have different ranges.

## What changed

- The new optional `source.supply.heat_minimum` is the lowest heating supply target: Hydronicus's curve, a fixed temperature, a warm-up, and the **Heating supply temperature** and **Warm-up temperature** numbers all stay at or above it, while cooling targets keep `minimum`.
  It defaults to `minimum`, lies from `minimum` to below `maximum`, and the Plant form's supply section sets it as **Minimum heating supply temperature**.
- `heat_temperature`, `heat_cold`, `heat_warm`, and `warm_up.temperature` are checked against the heating minimum.

Every 1.2.0 plant file imports and behaves as before.

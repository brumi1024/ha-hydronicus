# Hydronicus 0.5.0

This release makes the Plant's deliberate waits visible, so a dashboard can show what the equipment is waiting for and until when.

## Timers

- Every timer that holds equipment as it is now is published as a hold with its kind, its target, and when it ends.
  That covers valve opening and closing, a pump waiting for its valves, pump overrun, the source's minimum on and off times, post-run and feedback wait, the mode dwell, window delays, the thermostat's minimum on and off times, the condensation guard's minimum blocked time, and an exercise run.
- The **Status** sensor lists every hold in a new `holds` attribute, and each pump in `pump_operation` carries its switch's `reason` and its own `holds`.
- Each loop's flowing sensor adds `pump_reason` and the `holds` of the loop, its valves, and its pump; each demand sensor and the source's **Requested** sensor add their own `holds`.
- A switched pump that waits for a wanted loop's valves now says so, with the reason `waiting for its valves to open`, instead of showing no reason.
- The new attributes are not recorded in history.

See [Timers](../entities.md#timers) for every kind of hold.

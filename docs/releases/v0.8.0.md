# Hydronicus 0.8.0

This release closes a zone whose temperature suddenly falls or rises as an open window would, and reports a zone that does not warm or cool while it is served.

## Window detection

- A zone with `window_detection: true` in the plant file stops calling when its temperature falls 0.5 K within 10 minutes in heating, or rises as much in cooling.
- Its demand shows a reason such as `window open: temperature fell 0.6 K` and a `window_detected` timer, and the Status lists it in `blocked_zones`.
- It calls again once the temperature has stopped moving away for those 10 minutes, or after 30 minutes at most.
- A mapping changes `change`, `within`, and `max_duration`; window sensors and detection work together.

See [window detection](../plant-file.md#window-detection).

## Response check

- Every zone now raises the new `zone_not_responding` Repair when it has been served for 3 hours without its temperature moving 0.2 K toward the target.
  The Status turns `degraded` and its new `zones_not_responding` attribute says since when; control does not change.
- Only time with observed flow and, where the Plant has a source, the source observed running counts, so a pause neither fails a zone nor starts its count again.
- Progress counts from the coldest reading in heating and the warmest in cooling, so a slab that keeps cooling after its pump starts is no failure.
- `response_check: false` turns it off for a zone; a mapping changes `duration` and `progress`.
- A source with only a running sensor cannot show a hot water run, so that time counts as served.

See [the response check](../how-it-works.md#response-check) and [a zone does not respond](../troubleshooting.md#a-zone-does-not-respond).

## Shelly Modbus bridge

- The bridge script holds each setting to 50 writes a day, so a misbehaving automation cannot wear out the controller's memory.
  It is outside the release package and takes effect once loaded on the Shelly.

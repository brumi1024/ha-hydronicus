# Hydronicus 0.3.0

This release adds the safeguards that quality radiant zone controllers offer: frost protection, anti-seize exercise of pumps and valves, condensation switches, cooling limits, and a window-open inhibit.
It also fixes a command sent after unloading, a thermostat that showed heating while nothing heated, and valves that stalled while opening without a Repair.
Plants set up with 0.2.0 load as they are, unless their plant file sets a key this release removed.

## Safeguards

- Frost protection heats a zone whose coldest reading drops below 5 °C, even when its thermostat or the Plant's **Mode** is off and a window is open, and stops at 6 °C.
  It never acts while the Plant cools or with **Control equipment** off, and it applies only to a zone that a loop heats.
- Pumps and valves idle for 7 days are exercised through the normal sequence, one pump at a time, without asking the source for heat.
  An exercise gives way to demand at once and gives up on a valve that never opens.
- A pump or a loop can name a condensation switch, such as a dew point switch on the supply pipe, which blocks cooling at once while it reads on or unavailable.
- A loop with a surface temperature sensor stops cooling below its surface minimum, 20 °C by default, and a zone stops its cooling loops above its humidity limit, 70 % by default.
  Both come on top of the worst-case dew point guard, which stays the primary protection.
- A zone can list window sensors: after a window has been open for 60 seconds the zone stops calling, and it resumes 60 seconds after every window is closed.
- A digital thermostat now holds its decisions for 10 minutes by default, so a short call never opens a valve that closes again before its pump runs.
- Zone and area sensors count as stale after an hour instead of 30 minutes, which suits battery sensors that report only on change; condensation references still go stale after 30 minutes.

## Fixes

- Unloading or reloading a Plant could still send commands for a few seconds; stopping now cancels every command not yet sent.
- An evaluation that failed left the Plant waiting silently; it now raises a Repair and tries again after a minute.
- A zone's thermostat showed heating whenever the zone called, even with the Plant off; it now shows `preheating` while its valves open, `heating` or `cooling` while one of its loops flows, and `idle` otherwise.
- A valve entity that reports `opening` or `closing` is no longer taken for arrived: it is not ready until it shows open, and a valve that stalls on its way gets a Repair.
- Removing the source no longer leaves an empty device behind.
- A reconfigure and a zone edit open at the same time no longer overwrite each other; the second one to save stops and asks you to start again.
- Adding a zone to a Plant that failed to set up sets it up again.
- A stored state that cannot be read no longer stops the Plant from loading.

## Repairs and entities

- New Repairs: an evaluation that failed, a required zone sensor that has blocked its zone for 10 minutes, and a condensation input of a cooling loop that has been unusable for 10 minutes.
- Each loop has a runtime sensor and each zone a duty cycle sensor over the last 24 hours, counting only real flow while **Control equipment** is on; a zone near 100 % is undersized or poorly balanced.
- The Plant status reads `exercising` during an exercise and lists frost protection, and zones stopped by an open window appear among its blocked zones.
- The demand sensors no longer carry a `level` attribute.
- Diagnostics are no longer partly redacted, and show the stored Plant when it is not valid.

## Plant file

- New keys: `frost_protection` and `exercise` on the Plant; `condensation_switch` on pumps and loops; `surface_minimum` on loops; `windows`, `window_open_delay`, `window_close_delay`, and `max_humidity` on zones.
- **Plant and source** in **Reconfigure** has a **Protection** section for frost protection and the exercise, and the zone and loop forms carry the new zone and loop settings.
- Removed keys: the source's `strategy` and the digital thermostat's `proportional_band`; both return with flow temperature setpoints in a later release.

## Upgrade

Hydronicus requires Home Assistant 2026.9.0 or newer.

Install this release through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
The stored runtime state of 0.2.0 may not be read; the Plant then starts over as a new one would, and its **Mode** select reads off until you set it again.
A Plant whose stored configuration sets `strategy` or `proportional_band` loads as invalid with a Repair; replace it from a plant file without those keys through **Reconfigure**.
Frost protection and the exercise are on for every Plant after the update; turn them off in the **Protection** section if the plant must not act on its own.
A cooling loop's condensation guard now starts blocked, so the first cooling after a restart waits about 5 minutes.

## Rollback

Version 0.2.0 does not read the new plant file keys, so a Plant that uses them is invalid after a rollback.
To roll back, restore the Home Assistant backup taken before the upgrade, or remove the new keys first through **Reconfigure**.

Keep physical temperature, condensation, pressure, and flow safeguards independently active during any rollback.

## Known limitations

Hydronicus is a coordination layer, not a safety-rated controller, and its condensation guard exists only while Home Assistant runs; see [safety limits](../safety.md) for independent protection.
Heat-only loops are not exercised between the end of cooling and the next heating.
A frost zone whose heating loops are all unarmed or unavailable still switches the Plant to heat, although nothing heats.
There is still no automatic season selection in Hydronicus; [configuration](../configuration.md) has an automation recipe for it.

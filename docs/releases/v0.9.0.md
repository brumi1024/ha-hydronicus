# Hydronicus 0.9.0

This release completes the heating curve, keeps a home warm when a sensor is lost, and adds an away mode, an automatic heat and cool changeover, scheduled hot water with a disinfection cycle, and the Plant view a dashboard card reads.
Every new feature is optional; a Plant that sets none of them behaves as before, except for the fixes below.

## Fixes that change behaviour

- Frost protection keeps heating a zone whose reading drops out for a moment, until it is 1 K above the frost temperature, instead of stopping as soon as the reading returns.
- Frost protection heats even when the heating curve's outdoor sensor is lost, assuming 0 °C outdoors; before, the source was never asked for heat.
- A digital thermostat's start and stop deltas act exactly at their boundary; a configured 0.2 K delta behaved as 0.3 K with 0.1 K sensor steps.
  The default deltas act as before.
- A source found running after the stored state is lost is held for its minimum on time from the start Home Assistant shows, instead of being released inside it.
- The Plant form keeps the supply settings it does not show, such as `modes` and `step`, which it dropped when the supply section was saved.

## Heating curve

- `offset` shifts the heating target, and the source's new **Heating curve offset** number lets any user change it from -5 to 5 K.
- `outdoor_damping` filters the outdoor reading, so the curve does not follow every gust.
- `outdoor_fallback` names the outdoor temperature the curve uses while its sensor is lost; without it, a lost sensor still blocks ordinary heating.
  The new `outdoor_sensor_unusable` Repair says which applies.
- `heating_limit` keeps the source from heating above a damped outdoor temperature, with a 1 K hysteresis, while the zones still circulate.
- `room_influence` lets a lead zone shift the curve by its distance from its target, within a limit.
- The Status sensor's `heating_curve` attribute shows what the curve uses.

## When a sensor or Home Assistant is lost

- A zone's opt-in `sensor_fallback` heats it for a bounded share of each period while all its temperatures are lost, in heating only.
- The new **Heartbeat** sensor advances at least every minute while the Plant controls its equipment and evaluates without error, and lists the outputs it wants on, for a relay-level watchdog.
  [Keep relays on only while Hydronicus watches](../entities.md#keep-relays-on-only-while-hydronicus-watches) shows the automation.

## Away and automatic changeover

- The new **Away** switch and the `hydronicus.set_away` and `hydronicus.end_away` actions hold every digital thermostat at its away level, until an optional return time.
  A zone with a comfort schedule recovers in time for the return, and no manual target, preset, or schedule control changes.
  The Plant's `away` sets the targets of thermostats without an away preset, 16 °C and 30 °C by default.
- A Plant `changeover` adds `auto` to the **Mode** select, which chooses heat or cool from a day's average outdoor temperature, with a neutral band, at most one change in 12 hours by default, and the usual mode dwell.

## Hot water

- The DHW `schedule` holds the target a block's data names, such as `{target: 52}`, while the block runs.
- The DHW `disinfect` cycle raises the tank to a pasteurisation temperature, 60 °C every week by default, until its tank sensor has held it, gives up after a timeout and tries again a day later, and may wait for a window schedule.
  The source's new **Last disinfection** sensor records each success, and the `disinfect_failed` Repair reports a cycle that timed out.
  Keep a thermostatic mixing valve after the tank, since water that hot scalds at the taps.

## Plant view for dashboard cards

- The `hydronicus/plant_view` and `hydronicus/plant_view/subscribe` websocket commands return a versioned view of the whole Plant: its zones, loops, pumps, source, each zone's delivery path, timers, reasons, and Dry run proposals.
  See [the Plant view](../entities.md#the-plant-view-for-dashboards); no card is bundled.

## Updating

Stored state from 0.8.0 loads as it is.
To roll back to 0.8.0, first remove any new plant file key, such as `changeover`, `away`, `sensor_fallback`, or the supply's and DHW's new keys, since 0.8.0 refuses unknown keys.
See [plant file](../plant-file.md) for every key, and [research](../research/feature-roadmap.md) for the roadmap behind this release.
